## Aim

To demonstrate how various prompting techniques such as **Query Decomposition, Decision Making, Answer Engineering, Fact Check List, Tail Generation, Menu Actions, and Semantic Filtering** can be used to generate and improve content such as reports, articles, case studies, and product announcements using ChatGPT or similar AI models.

---

## Objective

The objectives of this experiment are:

* To understand different prompt patterns used for content generation.
* To generate an initial draft using a basic prompt.
* To improve content using structured prompting techniques.
* To compare basic and refined AI-generated outputs.
* To evaluate content based on coherence, creativity, accuracy, tone, and structure.

---

# Prompt Patterns Used

### 1. Query Decomposition

A complex task is divided into smaller and manageable subtasks.

**Example:**

> Analyze the electric vehicle market by separately discussing market growth, consumer trends, technology, challenges, and future opportunities.

---

### 2. Decision Making

The AI is asked to select the most suitable option from multiple alternatives.

**Example:**

> Choose the most suitable target customer segment from students, working professionals, and senior citizens for a new smart device. Give reasons for your choice.

---

### 3. Answer Engineering

Detailed instructions are provided about how the answer should be structured and formatted.

**Example:**

> Write the report using the following structure: Introduction, Current Trends, Challenges, Opportunities, Future Scope, and Conclusion. Use simple formal language and short paragraphs.

---

### 4. Fact Check List

The AI is instructed to verify factual information and avoid unsupported claims.

**Example:**

> Before generating the report, identify claims that require verification. Do not invent statistics, company names, market values, or technical specifications.

---

### 5. Tail Generation

The AI is asked to extend an existing response with logically connected additional content.

**Example:**

> Continue the report by adding future developments, possible challenges, and recommendations while maintaining the same writing style.

---

### 6. Menu Actions

The AI is given several possible actions and asked to select or perform the appropriate one.

**Example:**

> Choose one of the following actions: A) summarize the report, B) add market analysis, C) identify challenges, or D) write a conclusion.

---

### 7. Semantic Filter

The generated content is controlled according to specific requirements such as tone, audience, style, and complexity.

**Example:**

> Rewrite the content using a professional and educational tone. Remove unnecessary technical jargon and unsupported claims.

# Test Case Scenario 1

# Business Report on Market Trends

## Topic

### Electric Vehicle Market Trends

---

## Step 1 – Basic Prompt

### Prompt

```text
Write a short report about current electric vehicle market trends.
```

### Output – First Draft

Electric vehicles are becoming increasingly popular as an alternative to conventional petrol and diesel vehicles. The growth of electric vehicles is influenced by environmental awareness, technological developments, charging infrastructure, and improvements in battery technology.

Major trends include the development of better batteries, increased availability of charging stations, and the introduction of new electric vehicle models. Governments and automobile manufacturers are also supporting the transition toward cleaner transportation.

However, electric vehicles still face challenges such as high initial cost, charging availability, battery limitations, and consumer concerns about driving range.

Overall, electric vehicles are expected to play an important role in the future of transportation.

---

# Step 2 – Query Decomposition

### Prompt

```text
Create a business report on electric vehicle market trends.

Break the task into the following sections:
1. Introduction
2. Major market trends
3. Technology developments
4. Consumer trends
5. Major challenges
6. Future opportunities
7. Conclusion

Discuss each section separately using simple and professional language.
Do not invent specific statistics.
```

### Output – Improved Draft

## 1. Introduction

Electric vehicles (EVs) are vehicles that use electric motors for propulsion. They are becoming an important part of the automobile industry because of growing interest in cleaner transportation and advances in battery technology.

## 2. Major Market Trends

The electric vehicle market is experiencing increased interest from consumers and automobile manufacturers. Companies are introducing new EV models covering different vehicle categories. Charging infrastructure is also expanding in many regions.

## 3. Technology Developments

Battery technology is an important area of development. Manufacturers are working toward improving battery performance, charging speed, durability, and cost. Improvements in electronic control systems and connected vehicle technologies are also supporting EV development.

## 4. Consumer Trends

Consumers are increasingly considering factors such as running cost, vehicle range, charging convenience, environmental impact, and available features when selecting an electric vehicle.

## 5. Major Challenges

Important challenges include charging infrastructure, battery cost, charging time, battery recycling, and the availability of charging facilities in some locations.

## 6. Future Opportunities

The EV industry has opportunities in battery technology, charging infrastructure, energy management, vehicle software, and connected mobility services.

## 7. Conclusion

Electric vehicles are transforming the automobile industry. Continued development of batteries, charging infrastructure, and vehicle technologies can support their wider adoption.

---

# Step 3 – Decision Making

### Prompt

```text
For an electric vehicle company, choose the two most important areas for future investment from the following:

A. Battery technology
B. Charging infrastructure
C. Vehicle entertainment systems
D. Interior design

Select two areas and explain why they are important.
Use business-oriented reasoning.
```

### Output

The two important areas for future investment are:

### 1. Battery Technology

Battery technology directly affects important EV factors such as driving range, charging time, vehicle weight, cost, and overall performance. Improvements in battery technology can therefore improve the usability and attractiveness of electric vehicles.

### 2. Charging Infrastructure

Charging infrastructure is essential for convenient EV usage. Increasing the availability of charging stations can reduce charging-related concerns and support wider adoption of electric vehicles.

---

# Step 4 – Answer Engineering

### Prompt

```text
Prepare a professional business report on electric vehicle market trends.

Use the following structure:

Title
Executive Summary
Introduction
Major Market Trends
Technology Trends
Consumer Trends
Challenges
Future Opportunities
Recommendations
Conclusion

Requirements:
- Use simple professional English.
- Use headings and bullet points where appropriate.
- Keep the content suitable for a college-level business report.
- Do not invent statistics.
- Clearly separate facts from general observations.
- Keep the report concise and organized.
```

### Output

# Electric Vehicle Market Trends – Business Report

## Executive Summary

Electric vehicles are becoming an important part of the automobile industry. The market is influenced by improvements in battery technology, charging infrastructure, consumer awareness, and the development of connected vehicle technologies.

## Introduction

Electric vehicles use electric motors instead of conventional internal combustion engines for propulsion. Growing interest in sustainable transportation has increased attention toward EV technology.

## Major Market Trends

* Introduction of new EV models.
* Expansion of charging infrastructure.
* Development of improved battery systems.
* Increasing use of connected vehicle technologies.
* Growing interest in energy-efficient transportation.

## Technology Trends

Battery technology continues to be an important area of development. Manufacturers are focusing on battery efficiency, charging performance, durability, and cost reduction.

Vehicle software and electronic control systems are also becoming increasingly important in modern EVs.

## Consumer Trends

Consumers commonly consider:

* Vehicle range.
* Charging convenience.
* Purchase cost.
* Operating cost.
* Safety features.
* Technology and connectivity.

## Challenges

Major challenges include:

* Charging infrastructure availability.
* Battery cost.
* Charging time.
* Battery recycling.
* Availability of service facilities.

## Future Opportunities

Potential opportunities include:

* Advanced battery systems.
* Fast-charging technology.
* Smart charging.
* EV software.
* Battery management systems.
* Connected mobility.

## Recommendations

EV companies can focus on improving battery performance, expanding charging support, improving after-sales service, and developing reliable vehicle software.

## Conclusion

The electric vehicle market is developing through improvements in technology, infrastructure, and consumer acceptance. Continued innovation can support the growth of electric mobility.

---

# Step 5 – Fact Check List

### Prompt

```text
Review the electric vehicle report using the following fact-check list:

1. Identify statements that may require external verification.
2. Remove unsupported statistics.
3. Do not invent company names or market figures.
4. Separate general observations from verified facts.
5. Identify any claims that are too broad.
6. Suggest corrections where necessary.
```

### Output

| Check                   | Result                  |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------- |
| Unsupported statistics  | Removed                 |
| Invented company names  | None                    |
| Market figures          | Not included            |
| Technical claims        | Kept at a general level |
| Broad claims            | Rephrased               |
| Unsupported predictions | Avoided                 |

### Result

The report was modified to avoid unsupported statistics and specific claims that require external sources. This improves the reliability of the generated content.

---

# Step 6 – Tail Generation

### Prompt

```text
Continue the existing electric vehicle report by adding:

1. Future developments
2. Business opportunities
3. Possible risks
4. Recommendations

Maintain the same professional tone and avoid repeating previous information.
```

### Output

## Future Developments

Future EV development may focus on improved batteries, faster charging, better energy management, and increased integration between vehicles and digital services.

## Business Opportunities

Businesses can develop opportunities in charging infrastructure, battery management, EV maintenance, vehicle software, and energy management systems.

## Possible Risks

Companies may face challenges related to technology changes, infrastructure investment, supply chains, customer expectations, and competition.

## Recommendations

EV companies should focus on reliable technology, customer support, charging accessibility, and continuous improvement of battery and software systems.

---

# Step 7 – Semantic Filtering

### Prompt

```text
Rewrite the complete report using the following semantic filters:

Audience: College students and business readers
Tone: Professional and educational
Language: Simple English
Style: Clear and concise
Remove: Unnecessary technical jargon, unsupported statistics, exaggerated claims
Maintain: Important facts, logical structure, and readability
```

### Output

The final report was rewritten using simple professional language. Complex technical terms were reduced, unsupported claims were removed, and the information was organized into clear sections.

# Test Case Scenario 2

# Product Launch Announcement

## Topic

### Launch of a Smart Water Bottle

---

# Step 1 – Basic Prompt

### Prompt

```text
Write a product launch announcement for a smart water bottle.
```

### Output – First Draft

A new smart water bottle is being introduced to help users maintain better hydration habits. The bottle combines a convenient design with smart features to make everyday water consumption easier to manage.

It is suitable for students, professionals, and fitness-conscious users.

---

# Step 2 – Query Decomposition

### Prompt

```text
Create a product launch announcement for a smart water bottle.

Break the content into:
1. Product introduction
2. Main features
3. User benefits
4. Target customers
5. Product value
6. Call to action

Use simple promotional language.
Do not invent technical specifications such as battery capacity, temperature range, connectivity type, or water capacity.
```

### Output

## Product Introduction

A new smart water bottle is designed to support users in maintaining regular hydration habits.

## Main Features

* Smart hydration support.
* Convenient everyday design.
* Easy-to-use functionality.
* Suitable for different daily activities.

## User Benefits

The product can help users pay more attention to their water consumption and develop better hydration habits.

## Target Customers

The product is suitable for:

* College students.
* Working professionals.
* Fitness enthusiasts.
* People interested in healthy lifestyle habits.

## Product Value

The smart water bottle combines convenience and technology in a product designed for everyday use.

## Call to Action

Discover the smart water bottle and make hydration a more regular part of your daily routine.

---

# Step 3 – Decision Making

### Prompt

```text
Choose the most suitable primary target audience for a smart water bottle from:

A. College students
B. Office professionals
C. Professional athletes

Select one audience and explain the reason.
```

### Output

### Selected Audience: College Students

College students are a suitable primary audience because they frequently carry water bottles to classes, laboratories, libraries, and other activities. A convenient smart water bottle can support their daily hydration habits while fitting into their active student lifestyle.

---

# Step 4 – Menu Actions

### Prompt

```text
Choose one of the following actions for improving the product launch announcement:

A. Make it more professional
B. Add technical specifications
C. Make it suitable for college students
D. Add unsupported product claims

Choose the most appropriate action and explain why.
```

### Output

### Selected Action: C – Make it suitable for college students

The announcement should be adapted for college students because they are an appropriate target audience for an everyday portable water bottle. The language should focus on convenience, daily college activities, and healthy hydration habits.

---

# Step 5 – Answer Engineering

### Prompt

```text
Write a professional product launch announcement for a smart water bottle.

Use the following structure:

1. Catchy Title
2. Introduction
3. Key Features
4. Benefits
5. Target Audience
6. Why Choose the Product
7. Call to Action

Requirements:
- Use simple and attractive language.
- Target college students.
- Keep the announcement concise.
- Do not invent technical specifications.
- Do not make medical claims.
```

### Output

# Smart Hydration for Your Everyday College Life

## Introduction

Introducing a smart water bottle designed to make everyday hydration more convenient. It is created for students who want a simple way to stay mindful of their water consumption throughout a busy college day.

## Key Features

* Smart hydration support.
* Convenient for everyday use.
* Easy to carry.
* Suitable for college activities.

## Benefits

* Encourages regular hydration habits.
* Convenient for classes and campus activities.
* Supports an organized daily routine.
* Combines everyday convenience with smart functionality.

## Target Audience

The product is especially suitable for college students and young adults who maintain active daily schedules.

## Why Choose the Product?

The smart water bottle combines convenience, technology, and everyday usability in a single product designed for modern student lifestyles.

## Call to Action

Make hydration a simple part of your everyday routine. **Discover the Smart Water Bottle today!**

Fact-check the product announcement.

Check whether it contains:
1. Invented technical specifications.
2. Unsupported health claims.
3. Unverified performance claims.
4. Features that were not provided.

Rewrite the product announcement using these filters:

Audience: College students

# Advantages of Using Prompt Patterns

1. **Better Structure** – Complex content can be divided into smaller sections.
2. **Improved Accuracy** – Fact-check instructions reduce unsupported information.
3. **Audience Targeting** – Semantic filtering allows content to be adapted to a specific audience.
4. **Better Creativity** – Tail generation helps extend ideas and narratives.
5. **Improved Decision Making** – Decision prompts help select suitable alternatives.
6. **Consistent Formatting** – Answer engineering provides a fixed structure.
7. **Time Saving** – AI can quickly generate and refine multiple versions.
8. **Easy Customization** – Prompts can be modified according to the required tone, style, and purpose.
